> Served with bread from Grindelwald

I'm not an expert fondue taster, but I'd put this fondue's alcohol taste at a *medium* level. The first bite was kind of a shock because I wasn't used to it, and it definitely took me by surprise, but I got to enjoy it immensely pretty fast. The bread was amazing, soft and spongy, and we also ordered a side of French fries (sold separately) that paired pretty well with the fondue.
