> Pomodoro sauce, mozzarella cheese, and fresh basil

Very disappointing pizza. It took so long to get to my place that by the time I sat down to eat it, it was already soggy and cold; the dough was chewy and not pleasant. Even if we try to ignore the state in which it arrived, flavor-wise it wasn't there either: the following day, when I reheated it, it was still missing something.

Simplicity can be tasty, even amazing, but you have to do it right.
