> Prosciutto, burrata, basil, and almonds

I don't know if it was on purpose or not, but I liked that the pizza didn't have a perfectly circular shape and was a little bit amorphous instead.

The dough was airy at the crust, but the base was thin and almost crunchy, which I liked, and the almonds also helped with the crunchy texture. The sauce was saltier than I'd expect on a pizza, but I really liked it too.
