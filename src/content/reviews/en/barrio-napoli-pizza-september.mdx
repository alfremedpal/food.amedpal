<a href="/en/places/barrio-napoli" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Barrio Napoli</a> with another pizza of the month, this time for September 2026.

> A ricotta and fior di latte mozzarella base, with baby spinach, speck, sun-dried tomato, peach jam with habanero chili, and buffalo stracciatella.

I was excited about this pizza, but I came away a bit... not disappointed, but not delighted either. The combination of sweet and spicy coming from the peach and habanero jam sounded interesting, yet the jam's sweetness was just that: sweet. I couldn't really taste the peach. And the habanero came through *way* in the background and only at the end of each bite (I ended up adding some árbol chili oil just to feel something), which I suppose is to avoid scaring off people who aren't so used to eating a lot of spice. The speck, meh: indistinguishable from the prosciutto they use on their other pizzas. The sun-dried tomatoes were tasty, but I'm a big fan of those.

In the end, it's a good pizza because the dough and the ingredients are top quality as always, but being the pizza of the month is a "distinction" it couldn't quite live up to. And for the price the pizza of the month goes for, it's not worth ordering again.
