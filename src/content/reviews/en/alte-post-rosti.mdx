Pretty much a big hashbrown, but it was missing *a lot* of salt. Maybe that is just the way it is supposed to be eaten, but for me the flavor was extremely plain. The full portion was maybe a bit too much; I should have ordered it as a side so it wouldn't have felt overpriced for just potatoes with olive oil.
