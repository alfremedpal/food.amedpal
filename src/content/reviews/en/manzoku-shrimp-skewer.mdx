> Shrimp marinated for 24 hours, yuzu butter, chives
>
> 2 pcs

The marinade on the shrimp is _amazing_, and the charred bits give it a nice extra crunch and a deeper flavor profile. Unfortunately, it is borderline offensive that you only get two small shrimp, especially on such an ostentatious, big plate. Plus, the shrimp still had their tails on, and I find that annoying.
