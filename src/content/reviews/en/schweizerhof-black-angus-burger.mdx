> Brioche bun, smoked sauce, aged cheddar, red onion, salad, tomato, pickles, French fries

Probably one of the best burgers I have ever had. The meat was so tender, juicy, and flavorful that it just melted in your mouth. It came with a "smoked sauce" (I asked, and apparently it was just smoked mayo or something) that elevates the whole thing, _and_ it also goes great with the French fries, no ketchup needed (and to be honest, I'm not a big ketchup fan). The fries were ridiculously good too.
