> Mozzarella, pomodoro sauce, cherry tomatoes and portobello mushrooms, accompanied with basil-based pesto sauce, and olive oil

A nice surprise of a pizza since I'm not a fan of mushrooms. It smelled amazing, specifically the pesto, and it was very eye-catching and nice to look at (I swear the pizza looked so much better in person, I just don't know how to take pictures). Flavor-wise it was just a tad too salty, and I think the culprit was the portobello, which may have been over-seasoned. Luckily, the chili oil it came with balanced it out.

Even cold and with a chewy dough it was acceptable (blaming the delivery service), but it improved on reheating the next day.
