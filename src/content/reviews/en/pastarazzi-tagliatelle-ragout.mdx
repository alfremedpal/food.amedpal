> Beef ragout (OW beef), parmesan

I thought this was going to be a safe option, and in some ways it kind of was, but it was far from a good dish.

The pasta was slightly overcooked for my taste, and the beef ragout would have been better shredded instead of served in big cubes: chunks that were a bit stiff and hard to break down, *and* missing a lot of salt. The parmesan cracker on top, meant as a garnish, had gone soft long ago and felt leathery and chewy.

A barely-okay dish, fine if you're not up for trying something new.
