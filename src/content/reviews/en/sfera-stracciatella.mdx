The stracciatela sphere is a nice appetizer if you want something creamy to start your dinner with. However, because it is too creamy, it may clash and make your dinner feel repetitive if you later choose another cream-based main course, such as the <a href="/en/reviews/tavola-pizza" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Tavola Pizza</a>; which is a shame since they are both delicious, but it happened to me during my last visit.

> Cheese sphere wrapped in pizza dough with arugula, pesto, and garlic chips.

The pesto is nice and it has a nice color to it (which my pictures do not reflect), the dough is the same as they use for the pizzas (good), and I love the garlic chips it comes with. The arugula salad does add some bitterness that can tone down the creamy flavor in you case you find it overpowering.

If cut in quarters, I think it's great to share between four people, the perfect amount.
