> Tofu feta sauce, ichimi, chives, Eureka lemon, baby lettuce

New favorite appetizer?

First of all, from the moment it got to the table, I was drawn to it by just how pretty it looked; the colors and the textures were so nice. The size of the broccoli florets was perfect (well, almost; by the end you're left with the small ones, which are more awkward to pick up), and they were _so_ crunchy, I just didn't want to stop eating them. The tofu feta sauce was like a saltier, more seasoned aioli; I just wish there had been a little more of it.

They were also great with the sauce from the <a href="/en/reviews/manzoku-spicy-edamame" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">spicy edamame</a>.
