> A Mexican-style bean base, Italian sausage, and parmesan. Topped with spicy soppressata, croutons, and fried basil.

An interesting pizza for the month of November.

Flavor-wise, it wasn't bad; the bean base was actually really good. The problem is that it clashes hard with what you'd expect from a pizza. The Italian sausage and the soppressata are so good and make perfect sense on a pizza, but with the beans it's odd. Not bad, just weird, in a funny way even.

However, the major flaw of this pizza is that the beans are _heavy_ and they wet the dough _a lot_. So when you pick up a slice, its tip is already soft, and it just crumbles under its own weight into a soggy mess that I had to finish with cutlery.

And shockingly, the croutons were the highlight for me. Obviously bought and not made at the restaurant, but so well seasoned and super crunchy; they complemented the meats on the pizza really well. The fried basil was merely decorative, as it got lost among all the other textures.
