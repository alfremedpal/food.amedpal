> Romaine lettuce, bacon, croutons, Grana Padano

I don't remember ever having a Caesar salad with bacon before. Is this a common thing I had just been missing out on? Anyway, it was good.

The lettuce was mostly green, with barely any of those crunchier, whiter parts. It was well coated without feeling drowned in the dressing, and the bacon was *extremely* crispy (love that).
