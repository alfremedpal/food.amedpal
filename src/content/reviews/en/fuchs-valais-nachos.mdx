> Valais rye-nut chips with melted cheese

Probably the worst dish I had during my Switzerland trip, but hey, you can't win them all.

The "chips" were *so* chewy and leathery that eating them got tiring; halfway through the portion my jaw was literally hurting. Credit where it is due, the cheese itself tasted good, but since it was not emulsified (which honestly isn't that complex and would really make this more nacho-y), by the time it reached the table it was already pretty much solid and it added even more to that annoying, chewy texture. On top of that, having no cream, sauce, or any other topping was a real letdown.
