> Mozzarella di bufala, cherry tomatoes, prosciutto di Parma, Grana Padano, arugula, truffle oil

After a long day of hiking, that first bite into the pizza was almost orgasmic. The truffle flooded my mouth in the most amazing way; I still remember that feeling. By the second bite the truffle quickly loses its presence as you get used to it, but it is still very good. It is just that that first bite was incredible.
