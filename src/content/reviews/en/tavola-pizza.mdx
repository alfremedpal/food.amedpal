I've been visiting <a href="/en/places/tavola" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Tavola</a> more often recently, and it is mainly because of this pizza.

> White pizza with garlic paste, creamy ricotta and spinach base, aged mozzarella cheese, and white truffle oil.

I'm a sucker for garlic and truffle, and this pizza has a the right amount of both (a lot) in perfect harmony. Mixed with that *very* creamy paste, that slightly browned cheese, and that soft, chewy dough, the pizza almost melts into your mouth. The 8 slices are thin, yet they keep their shape when held. The crust has parmesan cheese shredded on top, which is a nice touch. What makes it stand out the most though, in my opinion, is the hypnotizing smell.
