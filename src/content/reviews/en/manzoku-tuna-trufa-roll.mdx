> Inside: tuna mix, cucumber, tempura flakes
>
> Outside: akami tuna, spicy mayo, truffle oil, serrano

Conflicted about this one. On one hand, you have a high-quality maki crafted with a lot of care and precision, but on the other, I feel like truffle is _not_ something that goes well with seafood in general. I was expecting the very fresh and light profile you usually get from a sushi roll, but the truffle was really overpowering and off-putting.

They do blowtorch your maki in front of you, which is cool, and it has a very nice, very slight crunch that I assume comes from the tempura flakes, but the spicy mayo and the serrano did not bring the heat I was looking for. I ended up adding some chiles toreados or the rest of the sauce from my <a href="/en/reviews/manzoku-spicy-edamame" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">spicy edamame</a>.
