> Avocado, scrambled eggs, bacon, swiss cheese, and chipotle mayo.

A nice, warm sandwich to start the day. The avocado is creamy, the bacon is good (although I'd prefer it a bit more on the crunchier side), and the amount of mayo is balanced with the cheesy, tender scrambled eggs. They all work nicely, but you gotta enjoy this warm.

My only "complaint" would be the bread, which is good, but I think it would be even better if it was just a tad more toasted to give the sandwich more structure. As of now, it is too soft and chewy for me.
