> Falafel, two kinds of beets, lemon-flavored pearl barley, figs

I don't usually go for vegan options (or even vegetarian ones, for that matter), but I did ask our waiter for a recommendation, specifically his favorite item on the menu. This was his suggestion, and I was surprised by how good it was.

The beet sauce was good; it had a *very* mild spicy undertone right at the end that I liked, and it worked in balance with the white barley sauce, which wasn't very lemony but still felt fresh and light. Both were very creamy.

The falafel was good at first, but it got tiring quickly, as it was quite sandy and a bit dry. It's obviously meant to be eaten with the two sauces, but the ratio was off: less falafel would have been ideal, since more sauce would probably have ruined the plating and made it look more like a soup.

The dehydrated leaf added a nice touch of green to the plate, and the beige-yellowish bits on top did help with the view as well. I'm not sure what they were, probably the second kind of beet? In any case, they were good too.
