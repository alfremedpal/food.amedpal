Sanbravo has never been one of my favorite places, I mean it is not bad, but it is far from the fancy or luxurious setting that they are trying to portray. Anyway, I was just there for a friend's birthday celebration and someone suggested I order the Crunchy shrimp tacos with chipotle. This person said they were so good that they had been ordering them for almost 5 years now. I decided to give them a shot.

## Plating

Lazy and overcrowded. They just threw a *bunch* of shredded carrot at the top to give them some volume I guess, but it only made them awkward to eat; I had to either use my hands constantly to get some carrot on my taco because otherwise it would just fall off, or accept my fate and not eat it all. Plus, so much carrot hid the actual shrimp.

## Flavor

Too basic. The tacos were very crunchy I'll give them that, but that's because the breading was so *excessive* to the point that you could barely taste the shrimp at all. The mayo-chipotle sauce was scarce, and at least according to the menu description the tacos also had some beans, but I did not feel them at all.

## Verdict

At a whooping $345 MXN, these 3 tacos feel almost like a ripoff. What ultimately saved them from a failing grade was the green sauce that they came with them because it added a nice acidity component.

I think they could be improved if they pickled the carrots, reduced the breading (or at least give us a larger tortilla so the shrimp didn't stick out so uncomfortably), and increase the amount mayo-chipotle sauce. Oh well.
