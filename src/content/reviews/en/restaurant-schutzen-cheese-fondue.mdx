> With bread, boiled potatoes, pearl onions, pickles, and mini corncob
>
> Price per person, min. 2 people

The fondue was good, with a _mild_ alcohol taste, and it came with lots of sides. The potatoes were _so_ soft and buttery that they practically melted in your mouth in combination with the cheese, a mix I didn't know I needed. The pickles were good as a side snack, but not so good with the actual cheese, just like the onions and the mini corncobs, which were my least favorite (but that's more a personal preference; maybe someone likes mini corncobs a lot and cheese with them could be life changing).

The bread, though, was _very_ disappointing. It was cut into bite sized pieces, which I thought was cute and smart, but they were **very hard**, rock-like even. I had to hold my piece of bread firmly with one hand and use the other to press the knife through it, otherwise it was impossible. And even then, many times the bread just broke and crumbled into inedible debris. I understand that the weather was cold and that may have had an impact, but even when the pieces came out they felt not soft, or not fresh at the very least.

And I didn't like that the **price is per person** and you have to pay for two people at a minimum; what if I just want a little bit and order something else? But as a party of two, this was our whole order, which I guess is cool because it means they do adjust the portions accordingly, but still.
