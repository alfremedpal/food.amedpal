> Spicy Szechuan sauce, garlic rayu, cilantro sprouts

In my opinion, the best spicy edamame in town.

The edamame themselves are soft and tender, but that sauce? Oh my. It is thick, glossy, and truly _spicy_. The first few edamame aren't that covered, so it doesn't feel so bad, but as you near the end you can see the _very_ generous pool of sauce just sitting at the bottom of the bowl; the remaining edamame are soaked, and that's when the fire kicks in. And because there _will_ be leftover sauce, you can save it for another dish and give it an extra punch.
