I walked into Casa Julia with low expectations and left wanting to come back
the next day. Their ham croquettes are some of the best I've had in a long
while.

The béchamel is spot on: creamy, almost molten as you bite in, yet it never
falls apart. The coating is thin and crisp, and the ham flavor is deep without
being salty.

**Best part:** the texture of the filling, and that they arrive piping hot.

**Room to improve:** the portion is too small for how good they are.

I'll be back, no question. And I'm ordering a double.
