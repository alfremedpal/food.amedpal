Probably the best crepe I've ever had.

It arrived at the perfect temperature, hot but not scalding, and the dough was so light it almost melted in my mouth. The sprinkled cinnamon and powdered sugar are a great combo that adds a really enjoyable aroma, and the mini apple slice it comes with is a nice little touch if you want to mix things up with something slightly acidic.

The actual chocolate inside, or chocly, was ridiculously good, with tiny chunks of I don't know what that add just the perfect amount of crunch without feeling weird or out of place. It was so good, in fact, that after I found out you can buy this chocolate spread on its own, I bought two jars.
