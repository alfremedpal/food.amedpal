The best carbonara I've ever had. It was so creamy and rich, and it had this very glossy, beautiful, intense yellow color that made it stand out. The pancetta was stupidly good; just the right amount of salty and crispy, it improved every bite substantially. Cooked perfectly, and obviously amazing with some extra parm.
