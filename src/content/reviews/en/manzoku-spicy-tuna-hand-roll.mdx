> Spicy tuna, katsuobushi, cucumber, unagi sauce

A good hand roll. The unagi sauce is sweet and tasty, and the cucumber adds just a little bit of freshness, but I couldn't taste the spice at all. You can order it with nori or a few other options as the wrapper; I went with the former, and it was good and not as overpowering as I thought it would be.

<Carousel
  images={["https://cdn.amedpal.com/food/manzoku-hand-roll-spicy-tuna-2.jpeg"]}

/>

It is a little bit small; it took me three bites to finish it, tops. But the price isn't so terrible, and as a _personal_ starter it's not that bad.
