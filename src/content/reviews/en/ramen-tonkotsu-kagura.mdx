I was craving a good tonkotsu and Kagura put one within reach. The result was
uneven, but with some very high points.

The broth is what saves the bowl: rich, savory, with the kind of depth only
long hours of simmering can give. The chashu melts and the egg was cooked
exactly right.

**Best part:** the broth, hands down.

**Room to improve:** the noodles arrived a touch overcooked and lost their bite
toward the end.

A place I'd return to — next time asking for the noodles *al dente*.
