> Fresh herbs · price per person

A fondue with a _light_ alcohol level, and very aromatic thanks to the herbs (duh), but it still tasted a little flat. I found myself needing to add a tiny pinch of salt and chili powder to every other bite to get that extra, missing kick.

The bread was okay, and it also came with boiled potatoes, but they weren't as soft or buttery as I expected.
