Since I’m a bit of a weirdo, I decided to open this space to share what I eat. The idea is to write about eating establishments and the dishes they serve, as well as the thoughts and feelings they evoke on me, covering everything from food quality, hospitality and service, to the price point. Who exactly am I sharing this space with? Basically, just myself. I doubt anyone will stumble upon this blog because:

* I don't plan on advertising or promoting it in any way.
  * It’s really just for me, a way to preserve and look back on my culinary experiences.
* And because, sadly, very few people read "conventional" websites anymore.
  * Social media has killed off small, personal corners of the internet like this one, since everything has to be centralized on the platforms that *tech bros* do us the "favor" of providing. Bleh.

But hey, it’s also a good excuse to:

* Practice my writing skills.
  * In both Spanish and English.
* Hone my web development and maintenance skills.

Anyway, if you happen to stumble upon this space and you are reading this (or any other post), I hope you enjoy it. If you have any comments, suggestions, complaints, or anything else, feel free to email me at info@amedpal.com.

Oh! And by the way, the entire site is open source. That means you can view the code behind it in my <a href="https://github.com/alfremedpal/food.amedpal" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">GitHub repository</a>.

Here goes nothing.
