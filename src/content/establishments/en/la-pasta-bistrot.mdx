We chose this place by chance, since it was literally just a couple of meters from our hotel. We loved it, and the next day a local recommended it to us, further validating that we'd made a really good choice.

## Food

Handmade, fresh pasta made that same day, and it shows. They have their "pasta laboratory" just a couple of meters from the restaurant, and you can watch them making all the pasta they'll use for the day. Pretty cool.

## Location & Atmosphere

We got to sit outside, on the pedestrian street with those big cobblestone roads surrounded by classic-looking downtown Italian buildings, and that was nice. Lots of activity, both at the tables and on the street, makes everything feel so alive.

Tables are too close together, though, so if you don't like sitting close enough to another table to feel like you could chip in on their conversation, this might not be for you; personally, I didn't mind.

## Service

Meh. I mean, it worked, because we could place our order and it didn't take long, but it felt like it was *just about* to fall into the "bad service" category. Nothing extraordinary.

## Price point

This was our first proper dinner after Switzerland, and compared to the prices over there, this even felt cheap. Trying to be more objective, I'd say La Pasta is fairly priced for the quality and experience you get.

## Verdict

Great pasta, lively mood, fairly priced. I'd love to go back.
