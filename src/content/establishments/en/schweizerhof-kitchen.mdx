Located inside the <a href="https://share.google/3VPLg9b0qN6q2WdGW" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Schweizerhof Zermatt Hotel</a>, this was the highlight of my trip to Switzerland, and nothing comes close.

## Food

The food was simply amazing; there are no other words for it. Every single dish we had was beyond incredible: from the quality of the ingredients, to the preparation, down to the plating and presentation.

They classify themselves as a "grill" restaurant, but honestly the menu is varied, ranging from salads, meats, and fish to vegetarian options and even pizza made in their very cool brick oven. You will definitely find something that catches your attention, and whatever it is, it will be good and you won't regret your choice.

## Location & Atmosphere

The vibe is very modern, maximalist, hunting lodge, so you'll see a *lot* of wood in different colors and shapes, hunting trophies, black stone and brick, and plenty of decor on every surface: books, lamps, photo frames. While this kind of style might be overwhelming for some, or even for me at times, I found it delightful, because everywhere you look there is something to appreciate.

My favorite part, though, was the kitchen, which looks directly into the main dining area. All night I was fixated on the hard-working chefs cooking right alongside us, grilling the meat, building and plating our order. They all looked (and most definitely are) incredibly professional and focused on delivering the best product. They also have a nice bar area if you are just looking for a drink.

There was a DJ in the back playing some nice tunes, though I have to admit the music was a bit too loud (am I getting old?).

## Service

Service was excellent, with the waiters always attentive to our needs and questions. It was *almost* as good as the food. It is clear that they take care and pride in every aspect.

## Price point

Our most expensive meal by far, but you know what, it was worth it. I didn't even mind the prices; I was too busy having a great time enjoying some amazing food.

## Verdict

The best experience of our trip, worth every single franc, and when I go back to Switzerland this is the place I would return to, no questions asked.
