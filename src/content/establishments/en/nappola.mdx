## Food

The menu is relatively short, but there are more than 10 pizza flavors to choose from. It also has some pasta and risotto options, as you'd expect from an Italian restaurant, but also some "not so common" offerings (or at least not common in "mainstream" Italian spots here in Mérida), like minestrone soup, chicken parm, and chicken al limone.

Pizzas are made in a brick oven, Neapolitan style (hence the name of the restaurant, duh), getting a nice char on the fluffy and airy crust. Good ingredient quality overall, and nice presentation of the dishes.

## Location & Atmosphere

I personally loved how the place looks: its green color palette is very appealing, and the complementary furniture really accentuates the whole vibe. It does look like they cut corners in some places, though; you can see trash bags stuck on the side of the staircase, and some of the overhead lamps could benefit from a shade to mellow out the brightness. Still, it's pretty.

The main dining area is upstairs, but if you can't climb stairs, you're stuck in the very limited space on the ground floor, unless you go out to the terrace, which may or may not be suitable depending on the weather. The reality is that it's quite a small place, and you can also tell when you go to the bathroom. There's just one bathroom for everyone, which wouldn't be so bad except that the sink is inside it; so if you're like me and just want to quickly wash your hands before sitting down to eat but somebody's using the restroom, you have to wait.

The restaurant is located in Temozón Norte, inside [Plaza Hípica](https://share.google/3FqY8gnmc9DHW3mcp). Getting there at a busy time of day can be tedious, as traffic usually builds up quite a bit, but the plaza itself is big enough, with plenty of parking spots. I am worried, however, that this could change in the near future, as it looks like more and more businesses are about to open there, including a pretty big BBQ place.

## Service

The waiters were okay, nothing out of the ordinary, and the main courses arrived in an acceptable amount of time. Drinks and desserts took a **very** long time to get to the table, though. Granted, when I visited we were a party of 8.

## Price point

Apart from the basic flavors and toppings, the pizzas are a little bit overpriced. Everything else is a mixed bag too, with some items that _look_ expensive, but I still have to try them out.

## Verdict

Really nice decor with good pizza and other non-mainstream options, but service can be slow and the bathroom situation is very limited. I really want to visit again, because I feel like Náppola could grow on me and become a semi-regular option.
