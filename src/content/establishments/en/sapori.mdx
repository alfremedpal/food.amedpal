I found out about this hotel through the Michelin Guide, and although it is "only" recommended (meaning no stars), it was well worth the visit.

## Food

Great Italian food, with high-quality ingredients making for high-quality dishes. The presentation and plating are well done; simple, but that is enough. There is enough variety on the menu to give you a reason to go back.

They also bring you some bread to start, which I always enjoy.

## Location & Atmosphere

The Sapori restaurant is located inside the luxurious <a href="https://www.victoria-jungfrau.ch/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Victoria Jungfrau Hotel</a> in Interlaken, and just like the hotel, it has this very historical, fancy, expensive, Bridgerton-like feeling that I liked. The details on the walls, columns, and ceilings are really pretty to look at and appreciate, and along with the warm lighting they help elevate the dining experience into an event that feels more intimate and cozy.

Actually getting inside was a bit confusing, though, as I didn't know whether I should enter through the hotel lobby or the "side" entrance marked as the actual restaurant. You would think the nice glass door with the "Sapori Restaurant" plaque would be a pretty obvious indication of the entryway, but as I went through that door I was met by what looked like a very confused host/restaurant manager, as if we were supposed to come in through the hotel lobby. Awkward interaction aside, everything else was fine: well-spaced tables (and plenty of them), good lighting throughout, and nice decor.

The bathrooms were weird, though. You need to go up some stairs to a very wide, long hallway (that eventually leads to the hotel's main lobby), and then down two sets of stairs to the bathrooms. It was kind of annoying that they felt so far away.

## Service

Good service, with a nice waiter who was very attentive all night and seemed genuinely interested in where we were from. Not a lot to say, just good service overall. No notes.

## Price point

Given that the restaurant is in a very fancy, quite expensive hotel, the prices on the menu reflected that level of luxury too. That said, for a very nice dinner at a place of this "level" with family or your significant other, the cost is worth it, as you will probably have a great time.

## Verdict

Really nice place, a bit pricey, but worth it for the lovely evening you'll have. You do **need a reservation**, though.
