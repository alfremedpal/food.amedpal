After a very busy and tiring morning of walking, sightseeing, and going up and down many mountains, we just felt like getting a quick bite in Lauterbrunnen. The restaurant at the Schützen hotel didn't look busy and the menu was promising.

## Food

Although the menu was very varied, we went for a fondue. We wanted to try other things, but the fondue portion (priced per person) was more than enough to fill us up.

## Location & Atmosphere

The place is nice. We sat on the terrace, which has a nice view of the mountains and the main road through Lauterbrunnen. The view was later blocked by a (very cool) retractable roof because it was raining, but hey, we didn't get wet!

## Service

Service was ok, nothing out of the ordinary, maybe a bit slow.

## Price point

Touristy prices, so a little bit high, but that was expected. I was not a fan of the fondue pricing being per person, though.

## Verdict

If you are in Lauterbrunnen, this is a solid place to eat. It's nice and the menu is varied, so there's something for everyone, but I'm willing to bet there are better places.
