## Food

I only had time to try two dishes, and they ranged from barely okay to just good, but the rest of the menu showed a lot of promise, especially their fall offerings (there was a pumpkin crème brûlée I was sad I didn't get to try).

## Location & Atmosphere

They have two locations in Lucerne (and I think even more elsewhere in Switzerland), and I visited the one in <a href="https://maps.app.goo.gl/u3KL6bxU5EpBMKR57" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Himmelrich</a>. I was simply delighted with the place: it has a kind of generic cozy vibe, but the neighborhood it sits in really makes it a great place to eat. Everything is so peaceful, as it's on a pedestrian street with a lovely park full of families enjoying their afternoon: people jogging, kids in strollers, cyclists... it was so nice.

## Service

Our waiter was nice, smiley, and polite. I asked him for recommendations and he was more than happy to suggest several things. The food did take *a lot* of time to come out, though, even though the place was mostly empty. We left what we thought was a very small tip (around 3 CHF), and the waiter seemed genuinely thankful for it, so maybe they don't get many tourists there. It's cool that we went to a more "local" spot.

## Price point

Expensive, but I think that's common for sit-down restaurants in Switzerland. There's really nothing more to say on this front.

## Verdict

Pastarazzi (at least the Himmelrich location) is a very peaceful, relaxing place to sit down and enjoy some decent food. The atmosphere and the neighborhood really outweigh the food, which wasn't amazing, but at least the menu looked promising.
