## Food

We made the whole trip to this place just for the fondue and didn't really look at the other offerings on the menu, so it's hard to judge the place as a whole. But if the rest of the items are as good as the fondue, then this is a great place to eat.

## Location & Atmosphere

The fondue was very good, sure, but the place? The **views**? At 1,322 m of altitude, looking down at Interlaken and Unterseen, I struggle to think of a restaurant with more breathtaking scenery. The air feels light and fresh, and the mountains give you comfort and peace as you look at them; it really does elevate the eating experience, especially an early dinner with the sun begging to set. That is, if you sit on the terrace.

I did not visit the indoor area.

## Service

Service was a bit of a letdown at the start, as there doesn't seem to be an obvious procedure for how the seating works, nor did anyone look like they wanted to give you a table: we wandered around the tables trying to get seated, or at least to get the attention of a staff member to help us, but no luck. We finally had to approach a group of waiters who were just standing there (not doing a lot, apparently) and ask if we could sit, and sure enough we were walked to our table.

Once the seating was sorted out, things improved. The waiter was attentive and nice, making conversation, cracking jokes and whatnot, and the food came out really fast. However, when we asked for the check and paid, leaving a small tip (as we had done elsewhere in Switzerland before), the waiter seemed insulted by the amount and scoffed at us, his whole attitude changing in a second.

## Price point

Tourist prices, *maybe* leaning a little bit into the overpriced category, but hey, the views are great. You do have to pay to go all the way up the mountain, though, so you might need to take that into consideration (unless your plan is to hike to the top, which is definitely something you can do).

## Verdict

If you are going to Interlaken, I'd say you *have* to go up Harder Kulm; it is worth it for the views alone. And if you are hungry, this is a good place to eat as well (if you don't mind some minor details with the service).
