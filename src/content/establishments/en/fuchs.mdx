We stayed in Zermatt for two days, and both times we hit Fuchs for a quick, small midday bite.

## Food

The sit-down menu is not that extensive, but I think the items are a nice mix of safe and "exotic" if you want to try something a bit out of the conventional (although sometimes they do not pay off, like the <a href="#" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Valais nachos</a>). They do have a separate to-go section, with more pastries and baked goods than on the "official" menu, but you can't order everything from the menu to go, which I thought was kind of silly. They do sell some of their dishes' ingredients separately, though, like the chocolate spread they use for the <a href="#" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">chocly crepe</a>.

## Location & Atmosphere

As you enter you can see the to-go area with a nice display of pastries and breads, but it wasn't obvious (at least to me) that you can go upstairs to the sit-down area. This sit-down area is a bit small and can definitely feel too crowded when every table is occupied, but nothing terrible. No unique or original decorations or vibes, but it is nice: simple and clean.

## Service

A bit slow, and the waiters did look like they were crumbling under pressure when the restaurant was full, but they were always polite and trying to keep a friendly face. Also, the dishes did not arrive at the same time, which was a bit annoying.

## Price point

Mixed bag. Some dishes seemed fair, but others felt *slightly* overpriced, though never egregiously so.

## Verdict

An almost too small restaurant/café with good sweet options, though we had bad luck with a savory one. Perfect for a quick bite.
