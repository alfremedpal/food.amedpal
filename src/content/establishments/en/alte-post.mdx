## Food

The Alte Post is a traditional Swiss food place, so if you are looking for something besides fondue, this is... an option. The portions are big and the prices sound fair considering it is Switzerland after all, and a very touristy town at that, but flavor-wise it could use a lot more. Maybe that is just the way Swiss cuisine is, maybe it was the Alte Post, maybe it was my palate, but the food was plain, borderline meh.

## Location & Atmosphere

Grindelwald is a *very* pretty town, so if you sit on the terrace you will probably have a good time and the dining experience will be nice even if the food is not to die for. However, we made the mistake of sitting inside. While the decorations and aesthetics are nice, very much that Swiss lodge kind of thing, the place was **riddled with flies**. I don't understand it. Why are there *so* many flies *inside*? They made eating *very* uncomfortable, as you had to constantly swat the little bugs away. Horrible.

## Service

Service worked. They took our order and the food got to our table relatively fast, but the waiters looked like they were annoyed by our presence; they definitely had a different attitude toward what looked like a table of locals. A bit annoying, and it made me feel out of place.

## Price point

Considering that eating in Switzerland is already expensive, I thought the price was fair-ish given the portion sizes.

## Verdict

Good portions, unsurprising food, but the place being overrun by flies is unjustifiable.
