Nico's pizza has this advertising campaign that goes something like "eat better pizza" or something, and honestly I can respect the grind and the work on trying to offer pizzas outside the commercial ones like Domino's or something, but unfortunately they don't always land. However, I think it might be because of their delivery service.

## Food

Ingredient quality is above average I'd say, and topping combinations are nice, but it is hard to be objective when my latest experience with Nico's pizza was one of a cold and soggy pizza due to delivery delay.

## Location & Atmosphere

I have only ordered as delivery, so I cannot review on this topic.

## Service

Communication for takeout and delivery is nice: they leverage WhatsApp's native menu feature that quickly lets you choose what to order. After deciding on what to eat, the person behind the phone usually answers fast and kindly, and keeps the conversation in a casual tone. So far so good.

The actual transportation of your food to your home is another story. Since they do not have their own delivery guys, they rely on some local group of guys that also deliver for other restaurants and delivery apps. This means that they may have other orders from a completely different restaurant and you might end up being the last on their route, resulting in a sad, soggy, and cold dinner experience.

## Price point

A little bit high priced, but the ingredients are worth it. In theory. Because I got my pizzas cold and soggy.

## Verdict

I'll be giving them the benefit of the doubt for now, because I do feel like my latest experience was soured by the delivery.
