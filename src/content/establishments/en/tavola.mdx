Tavola is a pretty good restaurant, and it has been becoming one of my favorite go-to places when I'm in the mood for some italian food. Everything about it just makes it a very desirable place to go and have a nice dinner in.

## Food

The food is really good. Tavola doesn't aim to be a traditonal italian place, but it doesn't feel like a butchered Americanized version of the classic dishes either. It sits in this weird space between what I think classic Italian food should feel like, and a more modern approach; but obviously without sacrificing the quality of the ingredients. I've had several dishes here (not all yet reviewed here) and they have all been enjoyable. 

The free bread they give you, some sliced white bread and focaccia both from sourdough, are one of my favorites; the perfect mix of crunch and chewyness that are improved when accompanied with the side of olive oil and balsamic vinegar they come with. I literally wanted to take a picture of it but I ate it all before I could.

## Location & Atmosphere

Located inside <a href="https://share.google/KWZa7I3KCsmDk1HcP" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">City32</a>, the surrounding area of the restaurant is already really nice (granted it feels a bit empty), and fancy. The air conditioner inside the mall is great, which makes Tavola's "outside" section a viable option. Inside the actual restaurant the mood is cozy with soft, warm lightning. There are plenty of tables with more than enough space among them, and you can see the amazing brick oven at the back from almost any place. Parking is a breeze and spacious, and validation is free on the weekends.

The background music did feel a tad too loud at times, but other than that the vibes are those of calmness and cozyness. Great for a date or a nice dinner with family or a small group friends, not so much for a "wild" evening drinking or a big party of people.

## Service

It is ok. Our server was attentive to our requests and needs and she was polite, but she did get our second round of drinks wrong (which honestly I didn't mind because she just mixed up our Coke Zero with regular Coke but some other folks might not like it). Always smiling and being nice, but I think it is because she already knows me from going there so often recently.

Nothing extraordinary, but far from bad.

## Price point

Fairly priced. It is not the cheapest italian food in the city, but for the quality of the ingredients and the atmosphere of both the restaurant itself and the place it is located in, I think the prices are reasonable.

During my last visit we were a group of 4, we had 3 appetizers and 2 entrees, a beer, a sangria and some Cokes, and the total bill came at just a tiny bit over $2,000 MXN.

## Verdict

A very good italian place with great food and even greater bread. I think it is kind of underrated or at least not so well known which is shame because I always have a great time when I visit.
