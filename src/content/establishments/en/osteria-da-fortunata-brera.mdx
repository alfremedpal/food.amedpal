I don't remember how we heard about this restaurant, I think it was through a TikTok video, but I'm glad we took the advice because it was worth the hype. And apparently it is so popular that they have several locations (is it a franchise?), not only in Milan but in other Italian cities as well.

So, this review is purely about the Brera location.

## Food

I forgot to take pictures and write down my thoughts on the dishes we ordered, but I do remember they were great. We had the Sciavatelli amatriciana, the tagliolini with pesto, and the tiramisu for dessert. Both pasta dishes were extremely fresh, as the pasta is made daily, and you can actually see the cooks prepping it before you enter the restaurant. Dessert was good, although nothing extravagant.

The menu is _extensive_, to the point that I felt a little bit overwhelmed by all the options, especially since you don't even get a physical menu and instead have to scan a QR code (bleh) that takes you to the restaurant's digital menu. There's just something so restrictive and artificial about seeing your food options on a phone screen; I don't like it.

## Location & Atmosphere

The vibes are cool, with a decor leaning into the industrial side of things. I thought it was funny how the tablecloths were just a giant piece of kraft paper, and then I thought it was even funnier when we asked for the check and they just wrote a number on that kraft paper and told us to go down to the cash register, give that number and pay (??). And obviously, right before you enter the restaurant, you can see the cooks making the pasta in real time, which adds to the experience.

## Service

The waiters were okay, but the food came out _really_ fast and hot, which is always appreciated. And again, the whole paying process, getting a number written down on your table and then going to the register to pay using that number as a reference, was odd... but funny!

## Price point

Fairly priced, no notes.

## Verdict

An extensive, digital-only menu that can feel overwhelming, but great pasta at a fair price; it's cool to see how they make the pasta as you enter.
