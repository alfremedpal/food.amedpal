## Food

The menu is extensive enough, and the options aren't just your classic rolls and some rice; you've got soups and ramen, hand rolls that feel almost like a Japanese version of a taco, and plenty of appetizers, both cold and hot.

Just by reading the dish descriptions you can _feel_ the labor that goes into each one, and the quality of the ingredients that make them up. Some dishes are pretty simple, but others involve some rather complex and interesting ingredients and techniques.

However, portions are usually small, so if you're a big eater you may need to order more than one (or two) items.

And this might be silly, but I hated that the drinks menu (also pretty extensive) was on a clipboard, and you had to turn it upside down to read the second page.

## Location & Atmosphere

If you sit at the bar on the ground floor it can feel a little bit small and uncomfortable, but you do get a view of the chefs prepping some dishes and of that beautiful display of bottles. On the upper floor things are more open and spacious, even for large groups. The decor is nice, and it feels a bit fancy and expensive, though not quite at the level of the food. Non-disposable chopsticks are always nice.

The music was funky; I liked it.

## Service

The waitstaff was incredible: extremely polite and professional, and very attentive to what was happening at the table. At some point I dropped my chopsticks on the floor, and before I could even turn to see where they had landed, a waiter was already handing me a new, clean pair.

Having said that, the food (and sometimes even the drinks) does come out a bit slow, but that's because it seems they take the preparation very personally and carefully, and want to craft every single dish as well as possible.

## Price point

**Expensive**. There's no other way to put it: this _is_ an expensive place with some very expensive dishes, especially when you look at some of the portion sizes. Is the quality good? Definitely, but sometimes not even that feels like a good enough reason.

## Verdict

Amazing quality, amazing service (although the food does take its sweet time), but rather expensive.
