Petit Délice is a really cute cafeteria with some excellent pastries, decent food, and a nice staff. I usually don't stay at the actual place to eat though (in fact, I don't remember the last time I sat down at a table), but rather I get it as takeout since it is so comfortably close to my home.

## Food

I don't go to Petit Délice for "actual food" but for the pastries, and let me tell you, they are great. Quality wise I feel like they are amazing in both ingredients and presentation: everything invites you to eat them. My favorites from them are the **almond croissant** and the **pain au chocolat**. Literally my go-to order alongside an **iced chocolate milk**, every single time.

My memories from breakfast and brunch dishes are not the fondest, but they were not bad either; they just pale in comparison to the pastries. Although, the <a href="/en/reviews/petit-delice-breakfast-sandwich" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">breakfast sandwich</a> has been becoming a favorite lately.

## Location & Atmosphere

The decoration is really cute and very French-like (I forgot to take pictures), it all looks very clean and polished. The outside area is very plain and doesn't reflect the restaurant's aesthetic at all, but it is fully shaded which is appreciated on a sunny and hot morning (which are most mornings).

Lots of places to park, but it can still get crowded and busy since the establishment is located at the very popular <a href="https://maps.app.goo.gl/3v9xpz8heTp5oCKm6" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Victory Platz</a>. And because the plaza is popular, the restaurant tends to be busy too, and because people *love* to talk at breakfast and brunch, it can get too loud at times (for me at least).

## Service

At least for takeout, the service is efficient and fast, and the staff is super nice. They already know me, but it didn't take them a lot of time to recognize me as a regular and remember my go-to order.

## Price point

Right now I think it is still fairly priced based on the quality of their food, but it is true that I have seen at least one price hike in the last couple of years. I'll have to keep monitoring prices.

## Verdict

Great place for some really nice pastries, but if you can get home quickly, I'd advise getting them as takeout to skip the loud crowds (even if the place itself is nice and cute).
